How solar works

From Sunlight to Switch.

A rooftop system turns daylight into power for your home in four simple stages.

Sunlight

Daylight falls on your roof.

Solar panels

Panels turn sunlight into DC electricity.

Inverter

It converts DC into AC power your home can use.

Home and grid

Your home uses it first. Extra power can go to the grid through net metering.

Government subsidy

Let the Government Help Power Your Home.

Eligible households that install rooftop solar can receive central government financial assistance. Here is how the subsidy works for homes.

1 kW system

₹30,000

Central subsidy at ₹30,000 per kW.

2 kW system

₹60,000

The first 2 kW earn ₹30,000 each.

Maximum subsidy

3 kW system and above

₹78,000

₹60,000 for 2 kW plus ₹18,000 for the 3rd kW. Larger systems get the same cap.

  • Up to 150 units a monthTypically a 1–2 kW system: ₹30,000 to ₹60,000
  • 150 to 300 units a monthTypically a 2–3 kW system: ₹60,000 to ₹78,000
  • Above 300 units a monthTypically above 3 kW: ₹78,000

Amounts are the central subsidy published under PM Surya Ghar for residential rooftop systems and may change. Any state or electricity-company benefits can differ by area, and approval and payout are decided by the government portal and your electricity company. Please confirm current terms on the official portal. This website is not a government site.

Central government scheme

PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana

Launched in February 2024 by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy to bring rooftop solar to one crore households by March 2027.

  • 1 crorehousehold target
  • ₹75,021 crscheme outlay
  • Up to 300free units a month is the scheme’s aim
  • About 7%loan interest for systems up to 3 kW

How to claim

From Application to Subsidy, Step by Step.

  1. Register on the national portal

    Choose your state and electricity company, then enter your consumer number and mobile number.

  2. Apply for rooftop solar

    Submit the application and wait for approval from your electricity company.

  3. Install with a registered vendor

    Select a vendor from the portal list and have the system installed on your roof.

  4. Net meter and inspection

    Apply for the net meter. After inspection you receive a commissioning certificate.

  5. Receive your subsidy

    Submit your bank details on the portal. The subsidy is paid directly to your bank account, usually within about 30 days.

Who can apply?

  • An Indian citizen with a house and a roof suitable for solar
  • A valid electricity connection in the household
  • No earlier solar subsidy taken for this property
  • Housing societies can also apply for common facilities, at ₹18,000 per kW up to 500 kW
