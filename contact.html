Before You Reach Out.

A few quick answers about getting in touch.

What should I share in my enquiry?

Your name, phone number and what you’re interested in are enough to start. Your city, roof or open space, and monthly electricity use help us understand your requirement faster.

Does the form send my details automatically?

No. It opens WhatsApp with your message already written, and your enquiry is delivered only when you press Send there.

Can I call instead?

Yes. Call +91 91319 10814 to speak with our team directly.

I’m not sure what I need. Should I still reach out?

Yes. Choose General Enquiry, tell us what you’re planning, and we’ll help you with the next step.
